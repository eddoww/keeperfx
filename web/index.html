Guide

How to get going, how online play works, and answers to the usual questions.

Getting started

  1. Get Dungeon Keeper. You need an original Dungeon Keeper or Dungeon Keeper Gold; the GOG release works.
  2. Choose its folder. The page reads the 16 files KeeperFX needs from it (and the soundtrack, if it's there) and keeps them in this browser. You only do this once per browser.
  3. Play. The campaign, free play levels, skirmish against the computer, saves and settings all work as in the desktop KeeperFX.

Controls and screen

Mouse
The game's hand follows the mouse, and the mouse is free to leave the game for the page, so Esc opens the game's menu at the first press. Push the pointer against the game's edge, or just past it, to scroll the view. Possessing a creature locks the mouse to the game so you can steer; Esc frees it there, a click on the game takes it back, and leaving the creature lets it go.
Lock mouse
The button above the game keeps the mouse in the game all the time, as the desktop game does: click the game to capture it, Esc releases it (so the game's menu takes a second press).
Left / Right
Left click digs, builds and picks up creatures; right click drops them or slaps.
F11
Fullscreen and back, like the Fullscreen button above the game. In fullscreen, resting the pointer at the top edge shows an exit button, and Esc leaves too (in Chrome and Edge you hold it, so a short press still reaches the game).
Fill tab
Hides the site around the game without going fullscreen.
Shift+C
Takes a screenshot; it is also saved to your downloads.

Touch screens

Tablets, and phones held sideways. The game's cursor jumps to your finger.

Tap
Left click: dig, build, pick up, press the game's buttons.
Drag
Left button held: mark a stretch of tiles to dig, or paint a room.
Hold
Right click, after a moment with the finger still: drop what the hand carries, slap, cancel. A quick tap with two fingers does the same, and Right on the side bar turns every tap into a right click until you press it again.
Two fingers
Drag to scroll the dungeon, pinch to zoom, twist to turn the view.
Side bar
Menu (Esc), Right, Keys (the keyboard, for save names and chat) and More: Space, chat, map, pause, Shift and Ctrl (held until pressed again), turn and zoom buttons, fullscreen, and hiding the bar.
Possession
Drag to look around, tap to attack (hold to keep attacking), the stick on the left to walk, and Leave to return to your dungeon.

Controllers

Any controller the browser knows (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, Steam Deck). Press a button once so the browser shows it to the page.

R stick
Moves the hand.
L stick
Scrolls the dungeon; walks in possession.
RT / LT
Left and right click.
D-pad
Jumps the hand between buttons in menus and the side panel.
A + D-pad
Zoom (up, down) and turn the view (left, right). A alone is Shift; B is Ctrl.
X / Y
Go to the fight; go to your Dungeon Heart.
LB / RB
In possession: previous and next ability.
L3
The map.
Start
The game menu. Back ends a won level (like Space); Start+Back quits.

Online play

Host a game

Press Host a game, name your lobby and choose whether it shows in the public list. You get a five-letter code and an invite link above the game. Up to four keepers can play, and others can watch as spectators.

Join a game

Pick a lobby on the Lobbies page, open an invite link, or type a code. The host chooses the map and the lobby settings (computer players, cheats, spectators) and starts the game.

Accounts

Optional. Guests can host and join. Sign in to keep your keeper name, talk in the tavern and enter the Hall of Fame. Your password never leaves your browser: it is turned into a key here, and only a hash of that key is stored.

How it works

This is KeeperFX's own multiplayer: every player runs the full game, and the keepers' orders are exchanged each turn, so all games stay identical. A lobby server on Cloudflare's network relays that traffic, starting close to whoever opens the lobby.

  • Everyone needs their own copy of Dungeon Keeper, picked in their own browser.
  • Online games are between browsers on this site; desktop KeeperFX uses a different connection, so the two can't meet.
  • The game runs at the pace of the slowest player, so a weak computer or connection slows everyone a little. If the host leaves, the others carry on on their own.
  • Keep the tab in front during a game: browsers slow down background tabs.
  • No one in the lobbies? The Keeper Klan Discord is where keepers find each other.

Watching games

Live games

Games in progress show under Live now on the home and Lobbies pages. Press Watch to join as a spectator, even halfway through: your game catches up with the others first. A game's watch link (#/watch/CODE) leads straight there. The host decides whether a lobby takes spectators.

Replays

Your game records every level you play, in this browser. When a level ends, sign in and press Publish replay to share it, or publish an older one from Replays. Each replay has its own page and link.

While you watch

The view follows the keeper whose game it is. Click their name at the top to switch keeper, scroll to look around on your own, and click the view to follow again. Esc opens the menu.

Why the game?

A replay is the keepers' orders, not a video: your own game plays them again. That is why it is small, and why watching also needs your Dungeon Keeper files and the same version of the game.

Questions

Is this an emulator?

No. It is KeeperFX, the open-source remake of the Dungeon Keeper engine, compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing from DOS or Windows runs here.

Why do I need the original game?

KeeperFX is a new engine, but a few of the original's files (palettes and sound banks) are not its own to give away. They stay on your computer: the page reads them from the folder you choose and never uploads them.

Can I play custom maps and campaigns?

Yes: the Workshop page lists the community's maps, map packs and campaigns from the KeeperFX Workshop. Add to my game keeps one in this browser and Play starts it. Multiplayer maps can be hosted online; players who join get the same map installed for them. Mods, creatures and tools still need desktop KeeperFX.

How does the Hall of Fame count?

When you win a level while signed in, your game reports the score, the time and the level statistics. Each level keeps your best score and your fastest win; your renown is the sum of your best scores. Wins as a guest are kept in this browser and are sent once you sign in. Games with cheats on don't count.

How much does it download?

The game and the data for the menus and the first levels come to about 110 MB; more loads as you reach new campaigns and levels. Your browser keeps it, so later visits start quickly.

Can I install it, or play without a connection?

Yes. Install (at the top of the page, or your browser's own install button) puts KeeperFX on your desktop or home screen in its own window. Under Offline play you can download the game data: the essentials (the game, its menus and the original campaign) or everything. After that, solo play works with no connection at all. Lobbies, the tavern and the Hall of Fame need one; victories you win offline are sent in when you are back online and signed in.

Which browsers work?

A current browser with WebGL 2: Chrome and Edge are tested; Firefox and Safari should work. On a computer, play with a mouse and keyboard or a controller. Tablets and phones (held sideways) work by touch; a phone's screen is small for Dungeon Keeper, so a tablet is nicer. You still need your Dungeon Keeper files on the device, or served to it (see ?dk= in the docs).

Something went wrong. How do I report it?

Use the Log button above the game (also offered when the game stops) and include the file with your report.

How do I start over?

Forget my DK files on the start screen removes the Dungeon Keeper files and music from this browser; clearing the site's data in your browser also removes saves and settings.
